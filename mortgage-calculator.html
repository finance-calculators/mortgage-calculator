How to Use This Mortgage Calculator

Enter the home price, your down payment, the loan term and the interest rate. Then add your annual property tax and homeowners insurance — the two costs most basic calculators leave out — and any monthly HOA fee. If your down payment is under 20%, the calculator automatically adds an estimate for private mortgage insurance (PMI). Your result is the payment you will actually see on your monthly statement, not just principal and interest.

How a Mortgage Payment Is Calculated

Lenders use one formula for every fixed-rate loan:

M = P × r(1 + r)n ÷ [ (1 + r)n − 1 ]

M is your monthly principal-and-interest payment, P is the loan amount (home price minus down payment), r is the monthly interest rate (your annual rate divided by 12), and n is the total number of payments (360 for a 30-year loan). Taxes, insurance, PMI and HOA dues are then added on top, because your lender collects them in escrow each month.

15-Year vs 30-Year Mortgage

On a $320,000 loan at 6.8%, a 30-year term costs $2,086/mo and $431,018 in total interest. A 15-year term raises the payment to $2,841/mo but cuts total interest to $191,306 — a saving of roughly $239,700. The 15-year loan is the better deal only if the higher payment still leaves you an emergency fund and room to invest.

5 Ways to Lower Your Monthly Payment

1. Increase your down payment — reaching 20% removes PMI entirely.
2. Improve your credit score before applying — even 0.5% off the rate saves tens of thousands. See our guide on the credit score needed to buy a house.
3. Compare at least 3 lenders — rate quotes for the same borrower commonly vary by 0.5% or more. Read how to get the best mortgage rate.
4. Choose a longer term — it lowers the payment (but raises total interest).
5. Pay extra principal later — $200/mo extra on the example above pays the loan off about 6.8 years early and saves roughly $113,437 in interest. Try it in our debt payoff calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a monthly mortgage payment calculated?

Your lender uses M = P × r(1+r)n ÷ [(1+r)n − 1]. Taxes, insurance and PMI are added on top of the principal-and-interest result.

How much is the mortgage payment on a $400,000 house?

With 20% down at 6.8% for 30 years: $2,086/mo principal and interest, or about $2,603/mo including typical taxes and insurance.

What is PMI and when can I remove it?

PMI is charged when your down payment is under 20% and typically costs 0.3%–1.5% of the loan per year. You can request removal at 80% loan-to-value; it is removed automatically at 78%.

Is a 15-year or 30-year mortgage better?

A 15-year mortgage saves about $239,700 of interest on a $320,000 loan at 6.8%, but the payment is $755/mo higher. Pick it only if your budget comfortably allows it.

How much does a 1% lower interest rate save?

On a $320,000, 30-year loan, 1% lower cuts the payment by roughly $200/mo and saves around $72,000 of interest over the loan's life.

Does this calculator include taxes and insurance?

Yes — enter your annual tax and insurance amounts and they are included in the monthly total, along with PMI when it applies and any HOA fee.

How much house can I afford on my salary?

Use the 28/36 rule: housing under 28% of gross monthly income, all debts under 36%. On a $90,000 salary, that caps your housing payment at about $2,100/mo including taxes and insurance.
